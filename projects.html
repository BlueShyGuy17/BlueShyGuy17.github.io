Xeno Busters

My first game ever. I made this as my first project in High School. It's a basic shooter where you control a little tank and fight a giant centipede. It was made in Construct 3, a game engine. The process finding out how to program the centipede was probably the most frustration I had ever faced in game dev, which is possibly due to it being my first-ever solo project. I had to familiarize myself with Construct 3, get help from the teacher, and get the centipede's segments to follow each other properly.

Come Back Builder!

My first game made in Puzzlescript. It's a rather basic game about pushing blocks to fix walls. There are some enemies in the game that try to block you from reaching certain areas. I found making levels in Puzzlescript to be a rather enjoyable experience. The code for how the mechanics worked was already present, which was helpful. My time spent was mainly level design, but I actually did make an original creation to put into the game: enemies.

Apollyon

My group's final project of our first year in Questar. It's a little 2D horror game where you play as a character who works at a mining company and discovers eldritch nonsense. I operated pretty much entirely as both a concept artist and a pixel artist while my teammates worked on how the game function.

Echo Chambers

A group project that I worked on a lot of the art for. There was another artist who helped to make other assets. A lot of art didn't make it into the game. It's a 2D platformer where you fight enemies through a dungeon. I worked on some concept art, but I more notably worked on the title screen, the shopkeeper screen, and the swords you'll see in the shop.
